Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

santasgotaux ("Santa's Got Aux") is a music and film review channel. This page explains what our YouTube publishing tool does with data.

What we access

Our internal uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to, and manage playlists on, our own YouTube channel only. It does not access, collect or store data about viewers or any other YouTube users.

What we store

The only data stored is the sign-in token for our own channel, kept privately on our own computer, and a list of the videos we have uploaded.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.

Google

Our use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
